Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about integrating AI into your enterprise operations? Find answers to common queries or reach out to our consulting team for a custom roadmap.

Get in touch
What makes your approach different?

We focus on practical, business-first AI solutions rather than chasing the latest technology trends. Every project starts with understanding your specific challenges and designing solutions that integrate naturally into your existing workflows and deliver measurable results.

Is AI consulting suitable for small businesses?

Yes — most of my work is with small and mid-sized teams. Solutions are scoped to your budget and complexity, so you're not paying for enterprise overhead you don't need.

How long does a typical project take?

A working prototype usually takes 1–3 weeks. Full deployment depends on scope, but most projects go from first call to live automation within 4–8 weeks.

Do you provide ongoing support after deployment?

Yes — every project includes a support window after launch, and ongoing plans are available if you'd like continued improvements and monitoring.

What industries do you work with?

Mostly service businesses — e-commerce, agencies, clinics, and local retailers — anywhere repetitive customer or back-office requests eat into your team's time.
